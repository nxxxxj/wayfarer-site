Pricing

Wayfarer is free. Your whole camera roll on the map, every photo matted, journeys stitched and played back, nothing leaving your phone. The plans are for what leaves it.

Every plan is yearly, and the biggest works out to less than a fancy coffee a month. The smallest is one coffee a year.

The App Store charges in the currency of the country your Apple ID belongs to.

  • Plans renew yearly through the App Store until you cancel, which you can do any time in your iPhone's Settings. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card.
  • A skin counts toward your plan the first time you share or finish a journey in it. Need more? Move up any time: Apple refunds what’s left of the old plan and starts the new one that day.
  • If a plan ends, everything you made stays: photos, routes, captions, tips, films. Journeys you finished keep their skin, for good. New ones use Graphite until you’re back.
  • The Wayfarer seal stays on the matte inside the app, as the stamp on a print. Everything a plan shares or exports leaves without it.
  • Prices are the App Store's for each country, as of 14 November 2026. Apple sets them from the US price, with local tax inside, and adjusts them when exchange rates move.
  • The rest is in the Terms of Use, in plain words first.
