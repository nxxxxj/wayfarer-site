Wayfarer

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

The short version: your photos never leave your phone unless you choose to share them. Wayfarer has no accounts, no analytics, no adverts, and no servers that hold anything about you. We could not look at your photos if we wanted to. We do not want to.

What Wayfarer reads on your device

Wayfarer asks for access to your photo library. It reads your photos and the details stored inside them — the date and the location where each was taken — to build your map. All of this happens on your phone. Nothing in your library is uploaded anywhere. You can give Wayfarer your whole library or only the photos you pick; it works either way.

What leaves your device, and why

Four narrow things cross the internet, each to make a feature work. None carries your name, your photos, or any account, because there is no account.

  • Map tiles. The map itself is drawn by Mapbox. Showing a map means requesting the tiles for the area on screen, which, like any internet request, includes your IP address. Mapbox's usage telemetry is switched off in Wayfarer.
  • Place names. To caption a place ("Fatih, Türkiye"), the coordinates of a photo cluster are sent to Mapbox's geocoding service, and to Apple's, for the name of the city. Coordinates only. The names are then kept on your device.
  • Weather. To show the conditions around a photograph, its date and coordinates are sent to Visual Crossing, a weather service. Nothing else is sent. Wayfarer keeps a copy of each answer in a small store of its own, filed by map area and day — never by you or your device — so the same place and day is not asked twice.
  • Routes. When you add transit to a journey, the coordinates of two stops are sent to Mapbox's directions service to draw the route between them.

Purchases

All purchases are made through Apple. Apple processes the payment; Wayfarer never sees your payment details. What you own is recorded by Apple against your Apple ID and read on your device.

iCloud

A few small settings — for example which map skins you chose — sync between your devices through your own iCloud account, using Apple's infrastructure. We never see them.

Sharing

Photos, frames and journeys leave your phone only when you share them, through the share sheet, to the people and apps you choose. A shared journey file contains the photos you put in it and the details you chose to show.

Hosted journals — coming

Wayfarer will offer optional hosted journals at wayfarer.pics: publish a journey and share it as a page on the web. Hosting needs an account, because we would then hold your published pages for you. The app itself never requires one, and everything on this page about your photos and your device stays true either way. When hosting launches, this policy will gain a section that names exactly what an account holds and how deletion works.

What we hold about you

Nothing. Wayfarer keeps no user data. The one thing it runs on the web is the store of weather answers named above, filed by map area and day, with nothing in it that points to a person or a device. This website is a static page. If you email us, we will have your email — and we will only use it to reply.

Children

Wayfarer is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Since we hold nothing about you, changes can only concern what the app sends to make features work — and we will name them plainly.
